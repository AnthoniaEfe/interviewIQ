Worked rule

A requirement is met (2) only when the CV shows that requirement: the skill, qualification, or experience itself, at about the level the requirement names. Related or adjacent experience is partial (1) at most. If the CV shows neither, the label is not evidenced (0). Use cannot judge only when the requirement is too vague to interpret or the CV text is unreadable.
